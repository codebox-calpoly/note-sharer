## Design Context

### Users
Poly Pages serves Cal Poly SLO students who need a campus-specific way to find, upload, and evaluate study resources. The primary users are undergraduates in high-enrollment courses; secondary users include graduate TAs, power contributors, and trusted student or staff moderators who keep the marketplace useful and academically honest.

### Brand Personality
The interface should feel academic, approachable, calm, and deliberate. It should support confidence and trust without feeling corporate, flashy, or punitive.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the repo's established Poly Pages UI system: warm off-white page backgrounds, paper-like surfaces, modest depth, sage accents, and warm apricot for primary actions. Moderator tools should feel like a focused operations surface rather than a marketing page.

### Design Principles
- Keep workflows dense enough for repeated moderator use, but leave enough whitespace to make risk and priority easy to scan.
- Preserve student anonymity in student-facing surfaces while exposing only necessary accountability context in moderator tools.
- Use status, timing, and consequences in copy so moderation decisions are concrete.
- Prefer restrained color and clear hierarchy over loud warning treatments.
- Make credit economy controls explicit and auditable.
